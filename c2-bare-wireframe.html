Product Anatomy

A guide to defining each part of the product anatomy

1 Business / Transformation Scope

What business critical missions and goals do we have, and what would need to occur for us to achieve them?

1.1 Use case definition

What are we trying to do? What value do we want to create?

111 Business Outcome

What result do we want to see in the world? What needs to be true, once this is delivered?

112 Business Processes or Use Case

What needs to happen, in order to deliver this outcome? What are the “jobs to be done”?

113 Persona

Who needs to make it happen?

1.2 Decision flow

What do we need to know, and what will we do with this information, once we have it?

121 Questions

What do we need to know (in order to take action?)

122 Activities

What specific activities may we need to take, to complete the process?

123 Decisions

What is the (possible and likely) decision tree in this situation?

124 Actions

What action might we feasibly take (once we have the above information)?
2 Data, Insights & Analytics Scope

What solutions will help us achieve our business goals, and what needs to be true about them?

2.1 Product and insight

What do we want to see on the screen, that will help us do our role more effectively?

211 User-facing product

Where do they need to do it (which digital product)?

212 KPIs

What insight do they need to do it?

2.2 Data and technology

What is required to enable the digital solution to work? What specific technology and tooling choices are the best fit for what we need to bring into existence via the digital product?

221 Data Assets

What underlying data is required to surface the needed KPIs? Which sources and/or systems does it come from?

222 Technology & tooling

What technology should power the solution we interacts with, and why this one?
