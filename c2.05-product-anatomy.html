Level 2|5 categories

Business context and value

›
  • 111Business process›

    What work needs to get done, from the broad business process through its sub-processes to the next level of detail in 121 Activity?

  • 112Persona›

    Whose job is it to perform these activities?

  • 113Pain point›

    What specific obstacle or unmet need makes the work within the business process harder, slower or less effective than it should be?

  • 114Business outcome›

    What should the work within the business process achieve that represents a meaningful result for the organisation and people within it?

  • 115Business metric›

    How will we know when the business outcome has been achieved, and what would success look like?

1.2

Level 2|9 categories

Decision flow

›
  • 121Activity›

    What activity should the analytics help carry out?

  • 122Question›

    What do we need to know to carry out the activity supported by the analytics?

  • 123Insight›

    What does the evidence show about the question being investigated? This is a “pre” insight, based on the data and evidence reviewed, informing the answer to the question.

  • 124Answer›

    What is the standard response to the question being investigated?

  • 125Decision›

    What course of action do we choose based on the answer?

  • 126Implication›

    What does the chosen course of action mean in practice?

  • 127Action›

    What concrete action do we carry out to implement the decision?

  • 128Effect›

    What happened as a result of the action taken?

  • 129Learning›

    What did we learn from the observed effect of the action? This is a “post” insight at the end of the decision flow, summarising everything learnt in the flow.
